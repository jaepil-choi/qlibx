# 182 — The loop is an EventLoop, and the agenda drops its role

**Date:** 2026-09-08. **Branch:** `redesign/component-eventloop` (campaign M2; plan
`.agent/plans/active/component-eventloop-redesign.md`). **Reviews:**
`docs/code-review/2026-09-08-four-readers-one-loop-and-the-missing-shapes.md` §2,
`docs/code-review/2026-09-08-flow-and-evidence-structure.md` §2,
`docs/code-review/2026-09-08-the-agenda-carries-a-role-nobody-chose.md` A–D.

## Why

`flow/loop.py` held the walk both run kinds share -- 54 lines that merged a frozen schedule
with the due items a callback mints, ordered by `(utc, priority, id)` -- and enforced nothing:
`run() -> object`, hooks that raised `NotImplementedError`, a subclass contract made of three
attributes assigned by hand. `SimulationFlow.run` recovered its own return type with an
`assert isinstance`; `DataModelFlow` carried a `type: ignore`. The dispatch branched on a string
field: `occurrence.role is OperationRole.STRATEGY_CALLBACK`, else `ValueError`. And that field
was the root of the other review's defect: a datamodel run's agenda froze as
`STRATEGY_CALLBACK` and folded that name into an identity of a run that never had a callback.

The owner named the pattern: what the loop does is advance one clock to the earliest of two
event sources and hand the event to a handler -- a discrete-event simulation loop, an
**EventLoop** -- and asked that the class say so. The naming rule in architecture §10 that
seemed to forbid it was, by the owner's own account, written to stop a first draft from
committing to a pattern prematurely, not to keep pattern names out of classes
(memory `architecture-intent-over-pattern`).

## What

**1/2 — `EventLoop` (`9024b554`).** `flow/loop.py` is
`EventLoop[EventT: Event, TraceT, ResultT](ABC)`:

- `Event` is a protocol (`sort_key`); `OccurrenceEvent` wraps a scheduled occurrence and
  `DueEvent` a due item (priority `-1`, ahead of any occurrence at the same instant). They
  replace `OperationEnvelope` and `DueExecutionEnvelope`.
- The constructor takes `schedule`, `start_cutoff`, `on_progress` and validates them; a
  subclass calls `super().__init__` instead of assigning three attributes.
- `run()` is the walk, written once and typed: it calls `start`, `pending`, `handle` and
  `finish`. `handle` and `finish` are abstract; `pending` defaults to `None`; `start` is an
  empty hook.
- `SimulationFlow` is `StrategyEventLoop`, `DataModelFlow` is `DataModelEventLoop`; their
  overrides are `handle`/`finish`/`pending`/`start` with concrete types, and the `assert
  isinstance` and `type: ignore` are gone. The phases are named for what they are:
  `CallbackHandler`, `ExecutionHandler`, `ValuationHandler`, `ComputeHandler`. Files keep
  their names until the layout move (M6).

**2/2 — no role, no provenance (this commit).**

- `OperationOccurrence(occurrence_id, local_instant)`. Its sort key's second term is the
  constant `SCHEDULED_PRIORITY = 0`; the role priority that ordered occurrences of different
  roles had no reachable input (an agenda was single-role by construction).
- `OperationAgenda(agenda_id, timezone, occurrences)`; `daily(agenda_id, sessions, at,
  timezone)`. `provenance` and `provenance_identity` -- computed, verified, carried, read by
  nothing -- are gone.
- `FrozenAgenda(agenda_id, occurrences, timezone, content_identity)`; `encoded()` folds the
  zone and the local instants, not a role. **Every strategy and datamodel identity changes.**
- `StrategyConfig(component, agenda_id)`; `_freeze_agenda(agenda, start, end)` no longer
  checks a role or re-proves offsets, because the one agenda is derived by `derived_agenda`
  and nowhere else.
- `StrategyEventLoop.handle` routes a scheduled event to the callback handler without a
  branch. The recorder `stage` labels the role values used to supply are the constants
  `CALLBACK_STAGE` / `VALUATION_STAGE` / `MONITORING_STAGE` in `flow/context.py`, with the
  same strings, so a record written before reads the same as one written after.
- `OperationRole` is gone from `vqapr.public`.

## Trade-offs

- **Identity break.** Deliberate and allowed (fast development stage). Existing run
  directories are not migrated; the sample and the showcases are regenerated by the campaign's
  merge gate rather than mapped.
- **`handle(event: EventT)` receives both `OccurrenceEvent` and `DueEvent`** through one union
  parameter, and the base's `run` carries two `type: ignore[arg-type]` where it hands each to
  `handle`: a `TypeVar` bound to a protocol cannot express "the subclass's union contains this
  member". The alternative -- two abstract methods, `handle_scheduled` and `handle_due` -- would
  force `DataModelEventLoop` to implement a due handler it never receives. One union and two
  ignores in the base was judged the smaller lie.
- **Stage labels stay strings.** A `StrEnum` for three labels read by nothing but the record
  writer would be a type with one consumer; the constants sit beside the tables they stamp.
- **The walkthrough steppers under `docs/walkthroughs/` still show `role`.** They are traces of
  earlier releases and are regenerated per release (memory
  `regenerate-spine-stepper-after-breaking-change`), not edited.
