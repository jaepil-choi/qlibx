# vqapr 0.13.1 — `check` answers for an `end` between a day's fill and its decision

Released 2026-09-10 from `develop`. One fix, record `237`, closing testbed report `docs/issues/099`.
Nothing breaking: no registered document, record or envelope changes shape.

## The ordering judgment asks about the occurrences inside the run (record `237`)

A daily strategy that reads a dataset stamped after the close decides after it (`agenda: {every: 1d,
at: "16:30"}`) and fills at the next session's close (`fill: {at: "15:30"}`). Its last data day has
a fill to include and a decision with nothing to fill at, so the one correct `end` lies between the
two -- `T16:00`, say. That `end` made `vqapr check` fail as a 500 `judgment.blocked`:

```
execution_ordering could not answer: ValueError: decision_time must not be after end_time
```

while an `end` after the decision was correctly named `execution.not_after_decision` (412). The
ordering judgment was the one reader of the run's agenda that took it as derived -- cut on
venue-local dates, a superset of `[start, end]` (`docs/issues/archive/069`) -- rather than sliced to
the horizon the way preflight freezes it and the run walks it. The `end` day's 16:30 decision reached
`select_target`, which refuses a decision after `end` by contract.

The judgment now asks about `inclusive_slice(start, end)`. An `end` between the last fill and the
last decision, or at the last fill, is answered clean; an `end` past the last decision is still the
412 that names the occurrence. `select_target`'s contract is unchanged, and so is the `check`
envelope: a judgment that could not answer still lands under `blocked`, not `failures`, with `ok:
false` -- a caller reading only `failures` should also read `blocked`.

## Migrating a 0.13.0 tree

Nothing to do. A run whose `end` was moved to after the last decision to get past the 500 can move
it back to between the last fill and the last decision; a design that was changed to decide before
the close (the report's workaround) can decide after it again.
